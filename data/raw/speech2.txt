Friends,

Technology has become part of almost every area of daily life. It helps people communicate, learn, create and solve practical problems. A useful invention is not valuable only because it is new; it becomes valuable when it makes life easier, safer or more productive.

We should approach technology with both curiosity and responsibility. Digital tools can provide access to information, but we must also check whether that information is reliable. Privacy, security and fairness deserve attention whenever new systems are designed.

Young people can play an important role in shaping this future. They can experiment, build projects and share ideas with others. With strong foundations and a willingness to keep learning, they can turn challenges into opportunities.
