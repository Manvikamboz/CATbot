My dear friends,

Leadership is not simply about giving instructions. It is about listening carefully, taking responsibility and helping a group move towards a shared purpose. A good leader recognises the strengths of others and creates an environment where everyone can contribute.

There will always be moments when plans do not work as expected. During such times, calm thinking is more useful than blame. We should understand the problem, consider different solutions and learn from the result. Honest feedback helps people and organisations improve.

Trust is built through consistent actions. When we keep our promises, treat people with respect and explain our decisions clearly, others can rely on us. These habits make teamwork stronger and help a community face difficult situations.
